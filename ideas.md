## Selected approach: Calming Clinical Ritual

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial healthcare design with references to Swiss International Typographic Style softened by Japanese ma-inspired whitespace and quiet hospitality.

### Core Principles
1. **Precision without sterility:** clinical information is clear and structured, but photography and copy remain personal.
2. **Whitespace as recovery:** generous negative space creates visual breath between dense content blocks.
3. **Editorial asymmetry:** split-screen compositions, offset cards, and vertical reading cues replace repetitive centered grids.
4. **Soft evidence:** thin rules, calm diagrams, and measured motion communicate trust without feeling corporate.

### Color Philosophy
The system starts with deep tidal ink for grounded trust, bone white for calm, eucalyptus for recovery, and a muted terracotta signal for warmth and human touch. The colors should feel like a quiet treatment room at 8am: cool enough to be precise, warm enough to stay.

### Layout Paradigm
Split-screen sticky sections. The left rail holds an anchored statement or image while the right side progresses through narrative modules, service protocols, and proof of care. On mobile, the rail becomes a compact lead-in and the rhythm stays editorial rather than collapsing into generic cards.

### Signature Elements
- Vertical chapter markers with small index numerals.
- Fine eucalyptus rules and terracotta “pulse” dots used as wayfinding accents.
- Oversized serif numerals and cropped documentary photography that interrupts the grid.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are quiet and useful. Hover states shift by a few pixels, reveal a second line of context, or brighten a rule. Buttons feel like invitations rather than sales prompts. Navigation maintains a progress cue so the visitor always knows where they are in the care story.

### Animation
Use short, ease-out transitions for buttons and links, 40–60ms staggered reveals for chapter items, and slow, barely perceptible image drift only in hero compositions. Avoid bouncing, looping, or overly dramatic entrance effects. Respect reduced-motion settings.

### Typography System
Headings use **Cormorant Garamond** in medium and semibold weights for a refined, human editorial voice. Body, navigation, metadata, and controls use **Manrope** with generous tracking and restrained weight contrast. Headline sizes should move from 4.8rem desktop to 3rem mobile, with body copy held near 1rem–1.1rem for clinical clarity.

### Brand Essence
**SVARA is a restorative wellness house in South Jakarta for people who want evidence-led treatments delivered with genuine quiet.**
Personality adjectives: observant, restorative, exacting.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are clear, intimate, and lightly poetic. CTAs are specific and low-pressure. Microcopy names what will happen next instead of promising transformation.

Example lines:
- “Care that gives your nervous system somewhere to land.”
- “See the room, meet the practitioner, choose your pace.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark is set in spaced small caps, paired with a custom circular “S” mark formed by two offset arcs—like a breath cycle interrupted and resumed. The mark is used as a compact compass in the navigation and as a visible favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Tidal Ink — #123C43**, an ownable blue-green black that carries both clinical depth and botanical calm.

### Site Architecture
Home, Our House, Treatments, The Method, Journal, Careers, and Contact.

### Asset Direction
Generate natural-light editorial photography: quiet treatment room, practitioner hands preparing tools, botanical steam ritual, textural close-up of linen and stone, and a candid portrait of a practitioner. No stock imagery, no visible logos, no sterile corporate posing.
